Tools for people who don't paste secrets into random websites

Practical utilities for sysadmins, mail admins and security engineers. Everything runs locally in your browser (the IP lookup is the one exception, and it only reads your connection's address). The site's Content-Security-Policy sets connect-src 'none', so pages can't send data anywhere even if they wanted to.

Tools

Email Header Analyzer

Paste raw headers. See every hop, the delay at each one, and SPF, DKIM, DMARC and ARC results, with red flags highlighted.

DMARC Report Viewer

Drop RUA aggregate reports (.xml, .gz, .zip) to see sources, pass rates and alignment failures for your domain.

Certificate & CSR Decoder

Decode PEM or DER: subject, SANs, key, extensions and fingerprints. Also checks the lifetime against the 200/100/47-day CA/B limits.

Cron → systemd Timer

Convert crontab lines to OnCalendar, with the cron day-of-month/day-of-week OR rule handled correctly. Generates the unit files.

What Is My IP

Your public IP, approximate location and timezone. For scripts: curl https://dorh.fyi/ip, plain text with no ads or tracking.

CIDR Toolkit

IPv4/IPv6 subnet calculator, overlap and containment checker, IP range → CIDR, and prefix aggregation.

Why another tool site?

Most free "checkers" are lead magnets. You paste email headers, certificates or DMARC reports, which often contain internal hostnames, IPs and addresses, into a vendor's server, and a sales email follows. These tools take the opposite approach: static pages, no backend, no analytics cookies. Open DevTools → Network while you use them and you'll see that no requests go out.
